Exercise 5.3.

Let \( f(X) \in k[X] \) be a polynomial of degree \( n \). Let \( K \) be its splitting field. Show that \[ [K:k] \mid n!. \]

Proof.

We proceed by induction. The case when \( n = 1 \) is clear. Suppose now we want to proceed from \( n \) to \( n+1 \). We split into two cases.

  1. Suppose \( f \) is irreducible. In this case, let \( \alpha \) be a root. Then \[ [k(\alpha):k] = n+1. \] Consider \( \frac{f}{X-\alpha} \) over \( k(\alpha) \). Then \( K \) is the splitting field of \( \frac{f}{X-\alpha} \) over \( k(\alpha) \). Hence, \[ [K:k] = [K:k(\alpha)][k(\alpha):k] \mid n! \cdot (n+1) = (n+1)!, \] by induction.

  2. Suppose \( f \) is not irreducible. In this case, let \( g \) be an irreducible factor. Then \( \deg(g) \leq n \). Let \( L \) be the splitting field of \( g \) over \( k \). Then \( K \) is the splitting field of \( \frac{f}{g} \) over \( L \). Hence, \[ [K:k] = [K:L][L:k] \mid a!b! \mid (n+1)!, \] where \( a = \deg g \), \( b = \deg \frac{f}{g} \), and \( a+b = n+1 \).
