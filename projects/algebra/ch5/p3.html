Exercise 5.3.

Let \( \alpha \) and \( \beta \) be two elements which are algebraic over \( F \). Let \( f(X)=\operatorname{Irr}(\alpha,F,X) \) and \( g(X)=\operatorname{Irr}(\beta,F,X) \). Suppose that \( \deg f \) and \( \deg g \) are relatively prime. Show that \( g \) is irreducible in the polynomial ring \( F(\alpha)[X] \).

Proof.

Since \( \alpha \) and \( \beta \) are algebraic, we have

\[ [F(\alpha):F]=\deg f \quad \text{and}\quad [F(\beta):F]=\deg g. \]

We also have two towers:

\[ [F(\alpha,\beta):F]=[F(\alpha,\beta):F(\alpha)][F(\alpha):F], \]

\[ [F(\alpha,\beta):F]=[F(\alpha,\beta):F(\beta)][F(\beta):F]. \]

So we see that \( \deg f\mid [F(\alpha,\beta):F] \) and \( \deg g\mid [F(\alpha,\beta):F] \). Since \( \deg f \) and \( \deg g \) are relatively prime,

\[ \deg f\cdot \deg g\mid [F(\alpha,\beta):F]. \]

We also have the inequality

\[ \begin{aligned} [F(\alpha,\beta):F] &= [F(\alpha,\beta):F(\alpha)][F(\alpha):F]\\ &\leq [F(\beta):F]\,[F(\alpha):F]\\ &= \deg f\cdot \deg g. \end{aligned} \]

Hence

\[ [F(\alpha,\beta):F]=\deg f\cdot \deg g. \]

Therefore

\[ [F(\alpha,\beta):F(\alpha)]=\deg g. \]

This means that the minimal polynomial of \( \beta \) over \( F(\alpha) \) has degree \( \deg g \). Since \( g \in F[X]\subseteq F(\alpha)[X] \) has \( \beta \) as a root, the minimal polynomial of \( \beta \) over \( F(\alpha) \) divides \( g \). The two polynomials have the same degree, so they are equal up to a nonzero scalar. Hence \(g\) is irreducible in \(F(\alpha)[X]\).
