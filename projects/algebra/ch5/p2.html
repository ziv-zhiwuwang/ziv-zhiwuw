Exercise 5.1.

Let \( E = F(\alpha) \), where \( \alpha \) is algebraic over \( F \) of odd degree. Show that \( E = F(\alpha^2) \).

Proof.

We have the trivial inclusion

\[ F(\alpha^2)\subseteq F(\alpha). \]

Since

\[ [F(\alpha):F]=[F(\alpha):F(\alpha^2)] [F(\alpha^2):F], \]

it suffices to show that \( [F(\alpha):F(\alpha^2)] \), i.e. the degree of the minimal polynomial of \( \alpha \) over \( F(\alpha^2) \), is \(1\). Since \( \alpha \) satisfies

\[ x^2-\alpha^2, \]

we conclude that

\[ [F(\alpha):F(\alpha^2)]\leq 2. \]

If it were \(2\), then the right-hand side of

\[ [F(\alpha):F]=[F(\alpha):F(\alpha^2)] [F(\alpha^2):F] \]

would be even. But by assumption the left-hand side \( [F(\alpha):F] \) is odd. This is a contradiction. Hence

\[ [F(\alpha):F(\alpha^2)]=1. \]

Therefore \(F(\alpha)=F(\alpha^2)\).
