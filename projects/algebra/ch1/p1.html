Exercise 1.1.

Show that every group of order \( \leq 5 \) is abelian.

Proof.

For \( n = 1,2,3,5 \), the group are cyclic. It remains to show the case when \( n = 4 \). Choose \( x \in G \), where \( x \neq e \). Suppose that \( \operatorname{ord}(x) = 4 \), then \( G \) is cyclic, and we are done. Suppose that there is no such element of order \( 4 \) in \( G \), then every element that is not \( e \) has order \( 2 \). We show that

\[ xy = yx \]

for all \( x,y \in G \). i.e. \( xyx^{-1}y^{-1} = e \). Since every element of \( G \) satisfies \( x^2 = e \), \( x = x^{-1} \). Therefore,

\[ \begin{aligned} (xy)(x^{-1})(y^{-1}) &= (xy)(xy)\\ &= (xy)^2\\ &= e \end{aligned} \]

Hence we complete the proof.
