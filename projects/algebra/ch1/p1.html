Problem

Let \( G \) be a group. Show that the identity element is unique.

Solution

Suppose \( e \) and \( e' \) are identity elements in \( G \).

Then \[ e \cdot e' = e' \quad \text{and} \quad e \cdot e' = e. \]

Hence \( e = e' \), so the identity is unique.
